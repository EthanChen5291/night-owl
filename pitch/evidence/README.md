# V5 fresh-test evidence snapshot

[`v5-fresh-test-provisional.json`](v5-fresh-test-provisional.json) is a byte-for-byte copy of the frozen as-run report from `vision/runs/modal-rat-v5-20260927/fresh_test_once_001/final_report.json`. It records rat AP50 0.615779 on 369 reviewed fresh frames, positive-clip rat AP50 0.703102, person AP50 0.492688, and exact PyTorch/ONNX AP parity. It also records 34 emitted events in the positive clip and zero over a 175.937-second negative clip. The [subsequent independent crop audit](../../vision/V5_FRESH_RESULTS.md) confirmed that all 34 saved positive-event crops target the visible plush. They are repeated firings across two complete coarse presence episodes, not independent encounters or formal push recall. The second episode's first alert was delayed 58.890 seconds. Source-only annotation QC is complete.

After the frozen test, source-only review of all 193 positive sampled frames found shifted and oversized rat reference boxes, including boxes too low on frames 149–153 and 157–162. Thirty-one source-estimated correction proposals are drafts, not approved replacement ground truth; none was adopted or rescored. The frozen labels and as-run predictions remain unchanged. The 0.615779 figure is the as-run score with known annotation defects, and this test did not establish the 0.90 rat-box target.

## Later fixed event test and live Pi loop

The separate [fixed formal event result](../../vision/V5_FORMAL_EVENT_RESULTS.md) failed: 18 of 19 source-reviewed plush appearances alerted, but 17 false events fired on a cap and shoe during 190.409 seconds without plush. All 60 emitted crops were independently audited. This test was recorded replay and did not POST to the API.

[`v5-live-run-stats.json`](v5-live-run-stats.json) and [`v5-live-integration-result.json`](v5-live-integration-result.json) are small copies of the later physical Pi observation summaries. The Pi processed 1,346 live frames in 89.859 seconds and saved six event crops, all visually confirmed as plush. One genuine event was relayed to the local API and accepted (HTTP 200), moving the served cell score .1297→.1618, sightings 1→2, and candidate rank 2→1. The other five saved events were not posted. The [local map screenshot](../assets/live-pi-map-20260927.png) is copied from the observation output. These files preserve one end-to-end prototype event, not general live detection reliability or a formal event-gate pass. No raw API store is included here.
