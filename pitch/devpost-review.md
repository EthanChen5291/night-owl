# Devpost review for Barn Owl

Read-only review of the public [Barn Owl Devpost page](https://devpost.com/software/barn-owl-vm9n3s), read 2026-09-26 at about 22:23 EDT. The page is accessible without login and lists `barn-owl.tech` under “Try it out”; the link redirected to HTTPS and returned HTTP 200. This review did not join the project, edit Devpost, or test the site's interactive app.

## Fix before judging

1. **Correct the infrared detector claim.** “Challenges” says the detector was trained on infrared footage, and “What it does” reads as though the night-vision node already recognizes rats on-device. Our detector training uses a dark **plush** in room light. Infrared recordings were excluded because the plush could not be labeled reliably. V4's new-room test scored 0.883 rat AP50, below the 0.90 target, and its recorded replay missed a parked plush and fired once on a dark case. V5 adapted using those clips as development material and reached 0.961 rat AP50 on its weakest selected development clip. **The frozen fresh V5 test measured 0.616 rat AP50 as run on 369 frames and did not establish the 0.90 target.** Completed source-only review of all 193 positive sampled frames found shifted and oversized reference boxes. Thirty-one proposed corrections are drafts; none was adopted or rescored, so no corrected AP exists. PyTorch and ONNX matched on the frozen labels. The exact V5 ONNX ran on a saved plush frame on the physical Pi at 60.23 ms median, confidence .931. A separate live-camera trial ingested frames without a plush target or API posts. **Positive plush detection from the live camera**, IR performance, and live-rat performance remain unverified. Suggested replacement for “Challenges”: “We trained a two-class YOLO11n detector on reviewed, room-lit plush footage. V5 improved on clips used for tuning, but its fresh test did not establish our 0.90 box target. Source-label QC found errors, and edge detection remains weak. IR and live-rat detection remain unvalidated.” Sources: [V4 new-room supplement](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v4-new-room-evaluation-20260927.zip), [V5 development candidate ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v5-development-candidate-20260927.zip) (`reports/metrics.json`, `reports/candidate_lock.json`, `reports/pi_saved_frame_smoke.json`), [frozen fresh report](evidence/v5-fresh-test-provisional.json), and [Pi live-bridge procedure](../vision/pi/LIVE_BRIDGE.md).

2. **Give the backtest its denominator.** The 19.5% versus 15.2% headline is the mean monthly share of *inspected lots with active signs* after each method ranks its top 50 **among cells swept that month**, over 119 months. “28% more rats” sounds like a population count or field deployment gain; the exported relative lift is 1.28 on this observed sample. Likewise, the quiet-block 1.8× figure compares active-sign shares (15.68% versus 8.81%) among swept cells, not numbers of rats. The building-model 2.2× figure is also conditional: 18.48% versus 8.46% active-sign share among previously uninspected buildings that were swept in the evaluation month. Suggested copy: “In a 119-month rolling backtest restricted to cells the city swept, Model B's top 50 averaged a 19.5% active-sign share versus 15.2% for prior 311 complaints, a 28% relative lift on the inspected sample.” Sources: [cell backtest export](../model/out/backtest.json), [cell implementation](../model/05_backtest.py), [building backtest](../model/out/building_backtest.json) and [implementation](../model/07_building_backtest.py).

3. **Name what Model B and Silence Score measure.** “What's actually there,” “rats likely but nobody is calling,” and “chance inspectors find rats” read like neighborhood prevalence. Model B estimates the share of swept lots with active signs at the cell level; it does not count rats in unswept cells. The public formula also appears as raw TeX with `pct!`, which may confuse readers. Replace it with plain text: “Silence Score = percentile of modeled active-sign risk minus percentile of 311 complaints per resident.” Say **few reports**, rather than **nobody calling**, unless a particular cell has zero calls. Sources: [model decisions](../model/README.md), [current cell export](../model/out/cells.json).

4. **Separate the working replay from the proposed owl network.** The page describes nodes that send only a “tiny event, not video” and suggests each detection changes what the model believes. The API event includes metadata **and a cropped image** (`crop_b64`), not a video stream. An accepted event updates a chosen Beta prior and may rerank existing sensor candidates; it does not retrain Model B. The proven map update came from an earlier **recorded-footage V2 replay on a Mac**. V4's reserved-clip replay did not POST to the API. Suggested copy: “In a recorded-footage demo, one detector event carrying a crop reached the local API, changed the cell's served score, and reranked an existing plan candidate. The physical live-camera-to-map path remains to be tested.” Sources: [event schema](../api/main.py), [posterior](../api/posterior.py), [store and reranking](../api/store.py), [current pitch script](script.md), and the [candidate ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v4-candidate-20260927.zip), which contains `weak_test_once/replay/summary.json`.

## Useful additions

- **Add detector results with their limits.** V4 scored rat AP50 **0.9258** on 59 adjacent frames sharing its training camera and session, then **0.8827** on 439 frozen new-room frames, below the 0.90 box target. Its recorded replay missed a parked plush and fired once on a dark case. V5 used those clips for tuning and scored **0.9608** on its weakest development clip. On 369 fresh frames, the **as-run** rat AP50 was **0.6158**; the positive clip alone scored **0.7031**, and combined person AP50 was **0.4927**. PyTorch and ONNX AP matched exactly on the frozen labels. Completed source-only QC found shifted and oversized rat reference boxes, including low boxes on frames 149–153 and 157–162. Thirty-one source-estimated correction proposals remain drafts; none was adopted or rescored. Preserve the as-run report, and do not treat 0.6158 as a corrected performance estimate. The as-run test did not establish the 0.90 target. The fresh saved replay emitted 34 events in the positive clip; independent crop review confirmed all 34 target the plush. It emitted zero events during 175.937 seconds of a negative clip. These are repeated firings across only two complete coarse presences, and the second presence first alerted 58.890 seconds after it began near the image edge. Two presences plus an under-three-minute negative reel cannot pass the formal 20-push / three-minute-negative gate. No corrected-label AP has been calculated. AP50 is a box-detection metric, not accuracy. Sources: [V4 candidate ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v4-candidate-20260927.zip) (`weak_test_once/verification/verification_report.json`), [V4 new-room ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v4-new-room-evaluation-20260927.zip) (`vision/runs/modal-rat-v4-20260926/new_room_final_once/`), [V5 development ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v5-development-candidate-20260927.zip) (`reports/metrics.json`), [fresh as-run report](evidence/v5-fresh-test-provisional.json), and [completed crop audit](../vision/V5_FRESH_RESULTS.md).
- **Use current, labeled demo media.** The public story currently shows one gallery image and no video link. The [rig photo](assets/physical-prototype.jpeg) shows the physical prototype. The [V5 recorded-development montage](v5-montage.md) contains real saved model-output stills, but its closing “fresh test pending” caption is now stale; pair it with the 0.616 as-run result and annotation-quality caveat if shown. The [V4 room-lit review sheet](media-v4/room-lit-review-contact-sheet.jpg) distinguishes manual labels from model predictions and shows the prior failure. Label replay media as recorded footage, not live-Pi or IR detection. The [V4 training clip](out/barn-owl-training-demo-v3.mp4) is a dated artifact whose caption predates the new-room test.
- **Qualify the equity claim.** The income and limited-English contrast describes **modeled silent cells**, not a causal result about residents or a measured rat census. The public page's 17.8× complaint versus 2.3× proactive active-sign comparison is supported by the [validation export](../model/out/validation.json), conditional on inspected lots. Keep that denominator near the claim. The “97% not swept” figure appears in [model documentation](../model/README.md); link or preserve its underlying table before presenting it as a precise public statistic. Add direct study links to the “rats stay local” line if retained.

V4 scored 0.883 rat AP50 on 439 new-room frames, below our 0.90 target. After adapting on those clips, V5 scored 0.961 on its weakest tuned clip. Its fresh test still did not establish the 0.90 target; the as-run AP50 was 0.616, and completed source-only QC found reference-box defects, but 31 proposed corrections were not adopted or rescored. The exact V5 model detected plush in a saved frame on the Pi; positive live-camera and IR performance remain unverified. An earlier recorded-footage V2 replay sent one event to the local API and updated the map. We will improve and retest before a field pilot.”
