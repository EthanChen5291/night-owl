# Devpost review for Barn Owl

Read-only review of the public [Barn Owl Devpost page](https://devpost.com/software/barn-owl-vm9n3s), read 2026-09-26 at about 22:23 EDT. The page is accessible without login and lists `barn-owl.tech` under “Try it out”; the link redirected to HTTPS and returned HTTP 200. This review did not join the project, edit Devpost, or test the site's interactive app.

## Fix before judging

1. **Correct the infrared detector claim.** “Challenges” says the detector was trained on infrared footage, and “What it does” reads as though the night-vision node already recognizes rats on-device. The V4 detector was trained on a dark **plush** in room light. Infrared recordings were excluded because the plush could not be labeled reliably. The exact ONNX model ran on a saved frame on the physical Pi (56.32 ms median). A later team-reported live-camera trial ingested frames and ran inference with no plush target and no API posts. This verifies live frame processing, but **positive plush detection from the live camera** and performance under IR or on live rats remain unverified. Suggested replacement for “Challenges”: “Pretrained detection mislabeled our plush prop. We trained a two-class YOLO11n detector on reviewed, room-lit camera footage. A separate new-room test scored 0.883 rat AP50, below our 0.90 box target; its recorded-video replay missed a parked plush and had one false dark-case alert. IR and live-rat performance remain untested.” Sources: [training results](../vision/TRAINING_RESULTS.md), [Pi live-bridge procedure](../vision/pi/LIVE_BRIDGE.md), [candidate bundle notes](../vision/artifacts/README.md), and the [new-room evaluation supplement](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v4-new-room-evaluation-20260927.zip). The Pi saved-frame report is inside the candidate ZIP at `pi_smoke_2026-09-27.json`; the new-room box report and event audit are inside the evaluation ZIP under `vision/runs/modal-rat-v4-20260926/new_room_final_once/`.

2. **Give the backtest its denominator.** The 19.5% versus 15.2% headline is the mean monthly share of *inspected lots with active signs* after each method ranks its top 50 **among cells swept that month**, over 119 months. “28% more rats” sounds like a population count or field deployment gain; the exported relative lift is 1.28 on this observed sample. Likewise, the quiet-block 1.8× figure compares active-sign shares (15.68% versus 8.81%) among swept cells, not numbers of rats. The building-model 2.2× figure is also conditional: 18.48% versus 8.46% active-sign share among previously uninspected buildings that were swept in the evaluation month. Suggested copy: “In a 119-month rolling backtest restricted to cells the city swept, Model B's top 50 averaged a 19.5% active-sign share versus 15.2% for prior 311 complaints, a 28% relative lift on the inspected sample.” Sources: [cell backtest export](../model/out/backtest.json), [cell implementation](../model/05_backtest.py), [building backtest](../model/out/building_backtest.json) and [implementation](../model/07_building_backtest.py).

3. **Name what Model B and Silence Score measure.** “What's actually there,” “rats likely but nobody is calling,” and “chance inspectors find rats” read like neighborhood prevalence. Model B estimates the share of swept lots with active signs at the cell level; it does not count rats in unswept cells. The public formula also appears as raw TeX with `pct!`, which may confuse readers. Replace it with plain text: “Silence Score = percentile of modeled active-sign risk minus percentile of 311 complaints per resident.” Say **few reports**, rather than **nobody calling**, unless a particular cell has zero calls. Sources: [model decisions](../model/README.md), [current cell export](../model/out/cells.json).

4. **Separate the working replay from the proposed owl network.** The page describes nodes that send only a “tiny event, not video” and suggests each detection changes what the model believes. The API event includes metadata **and a cropped image** (`crop_b64`), not a video stream. An accepted event updates a chosen Beta prior and may rerank existing sensor candidates; it does not retrain Model B. The proven map update came from an earlier **recorded-footage V2 replay on a Mac**. V4's reserved-clip replay did not POST to the API. Suggested copy: “In a recorded-footage demo, one detector event carrying a crop reached the local API, changed the cell's served score, and reranked an existing plan candidate. The physical live-camera-to-map path remains to be tested.” Sources: [event schema](../api/main.py), [posterior](../api/posterior.py), [store and reranking](../api/store.py), [current pitch script](script.md), and the [candidate ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v4-candidate-20260927.zip), which contains `weak_test_once/replay/summary.json`.

## Useful additions

- **Add both locked detector results with their limits.** The selected V4 ONNX scored rat AP50 **0.9258** and person AP50 **0.8764** on 59 reserved frames from four previously unused clips. Those clips shared the training camera, table, and session. A frozen new-room test scored rat AP50 **0.8827** on 439 reviewed frames, below the 0.90 target. Its full-video replay produced nine plush events and one false alert during the moving clip, zero events during one parked-plush exposure, and zero events over 181.21 seconds of a people-only clip. AP50 is a box-detection metric, not accuracy or event recall. Sources: [candidate ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v4-candidate-20260927.zip) (`weak_test_once/verification/verification_report.json`) and [new-room evaluation ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v4-new-room-evaluation-20260927.zip) (`vision/runs/modal-rat-v4-20260926/new_room_final_once/verification/verification_report.json` and `audit/independent_review.md`).
- **Use the demo media.** The public story currently shows one gallery image and no video link. The [15.6-second V4 training clip](out/barn-owl-training-demo-v3.mp4) shows the actual training log and ONNX predictions on a validation clip, with the same-session test caveat. The [rig photo](assets/physical-prototype.jpeg) shows the physical prototype. Label both as prototype/recorded footage if added.
- **Qualify the equity claim.** The income and limited-English contrast describes **modeled silent cells**, not a causal result about residents or a measured rat census. The public page's 17.8× complaint versus 2.3× proactive active-sign comparison is supported by the [validation export](../model/out/validation.json), conditional on inspected lots. Keep that denominator near the claim. The “97% not swept” figure appears in [model documentation](../model/README.md); link or preserve its underlying table before presenting it as a precise public statistic. Add direct study links to the “rats stay local” line if retained.

## Proposed short detector paragraph

"Our physical prototype uses a Pi 5 camera and PIR sensor. We trained YOLO11n to detect a dark plush rat in **room-lit** footage and exported it to ONNX. A locked test scored 0.926 rat AP50 on 59 reserved frames from adjacent clips recorded in the same camera session; an independent new-room test scored 0.883 on 439 reviewed frames, below our 0.90 target. Recorded replay found moving plush events but missed a parked plush and generated one false case alert. The exact model ran on a saved frame on the Pi, and a live-camera trial processed frames without a plush target. **Positive plush detection from the live camera** and IR performance remain unverified. A separate recorded-footage replay showed an event reaching the local API and updating the map.”
