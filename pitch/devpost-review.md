# Devpost review for Barn Owl

Read-only review of the public [Barn Owl Devpost page](https://devpost.com/software/barn-owl-vm9n3s), read 2026-09-26 at about 22:23 EDT. The page is accessible without login and lists `barn-owl.tech` under “Try it out”; the link redirected to HTTPS and returned HTTP 200. This review did not join the project, edit Devpost, or test the site's interactive app.

## Fix before judging

1. **Correct the infrared detector claim.** “Challenges” says the detector was trained on infrared footage, and “What it does” reads as though the night-vision node already recognizes rats on-device. Our detector training uses a dark **plush** in room light. Infrared recordings were excluded because the plush could not be labeled reliably. V4's new-room test scored 0.883 rat AP50, below the 0.90 target. V5 adapted using those clips as development material and reached 0.961 on its weakest selected development clip. **The frozen fresh V5 box test measured 0.616 rat AP50 as run on 369 frames and did not establish the 0.90 target.** Completed source-only review found shifted and oversized reference boxes. Thirty-one proposed corrections were neither adopted nor rescored, so no corrected AP exists. The fixed formal event test also **failed**: 18 of 19 reviewed plush appearances alerted, but 17 false events fired on a cap and shoe in 190.409 seconds without plush. One genuine room-lit plush event did travel from the live Pi camera through the local API to the map. IR and wild-rat performance remain unverified. Suggested replacement for “Challenges”: “We trained a two-class YOLO11n detector on reviewed, room-lit plush footage. A live Pi plush event reached our local map, but fixed recorded testing exposed false alerts on a cap and shoe, and fresh box testing did not establish our 0.90 target. IR and wild-rat detection remain unvalidated.” Sources: [V4 new-room supplement](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v4-new-room-evaluation-20260927.zip), [V5 development candidate ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v5-development-candidate-20260927.zip), [fresh results](../vision/V5_FRESH_RESULTS.md), [formal event results](../vision/V5_FORMAL_EVENT_RESULTS.md), and [live integration result](evidence/v5-live-integration-result.json).

2. **Give the backtest its denominator.** The 19.5% versus 15.2% headline is the mean monthly share of *inspected lots with active signs* after each method ranks its top 50 **among cells swept that month**, over 119 months. “28% more rats” sounds like a population count or field deployment gain; the exported relative lift is 1.28 on this observed sample. Likewise, the quiet-block 1.8× figure compares active-sign shares (15.68% versus 8.81%) among swept cells, not numbers of rats. The building-model 2.2× figure is also conditional: 18.48% versus 8.46% active-sign share among previously uninspected buildings that were swept in the evaluation month. Suggested copy: “In a 119-month rolling backtest restricted to cells the city swept, Model B's top 50 averaged a 19.5% active-sign share versus 15.2% for prior 311 complaints, a 28% relative lift on the inspected sample.” Sources: [cell backtest export](../model/out/backtest.json), [cell implementation](../model/05_backtest.py), [building backtest](../model/out/building_backtest.json) and [implementation](../model/07_building_backtest.py).

3. **Name what Model B and Silence Score measure.** “What's actually there,” “rats likely but nobody is calling,” and “chance inspectors find rats” read like neighborhood prevalence. Model B estimates the share of swept lots with active signs at the cell level; it does not count rats in unswept cells. The public formula also appears as raw TeX with `pct!`, which may confuse readers. Replace it with plain text: “Silence Score = percentile of modeled active-sign risk minus percentile of 311 complaints per resident.” Say **few reports**, rather than **nobody calling**, unless a particular cell has zero calls. Sources: [model decisions](../model/README.md), [current cell export](../model/out/cells.json).

4. **Describe the verified live loop precisely.** The page says nodes send only a “tiny event, not video” and suggests each detection changes what the model believes. The API event includes metadata **and a cropped image** (`crop_b64`), not a video stream. An accepted event updates a chosen Beta prior and may rerank existing sensor candidates; it does not retrain Model B. In a 89.859-second live V5 Pi observation of a room-lit plush, six saved crops showed the plush. Exactly one genuine event was relayed to the local API and accepted; the map changed from one sighting to two, site rank 2 to 1, and score .1297 to .1618. The other five saved events were not posted. This verifies one prototype loop, not routine night or field operation. Suggested copy: “A live Pi camera detected our room-lit plush prop. We sent one crop event to the local API, which updated the served cell score and reranked a sensor candidate on the map. The event updates a chosen prior rather than retraining Model B.” Sources: [event schema](../api/main.py), [posterior](../api/posterior.py), [store and reranking](../api/store.py), [live integration result](evidence/v5-live-integration-result.json), [live run stats](evidence/v5-live-run-stats.json), and [map screenshot](assets/live-pi-map-20260927.png).

## Useful additions

- **Add detector results with their limits.** V4 scored rat AP50 **0.9258** on 59 same-session adjacent frames, then **0.8827** on 439 frozen new-room frames, below the 0.90 box target. V5 used those clips for tuning and scored **0.9608** on its weakest development clip. On 369 fresh frames, the **as-run** rat AP50 was **0.6158**; completed source-only QC found shifted and oversized reference boxes. Thirty-one proposed corrections remain drafts, so there is no corrected AP. The box target was not established. A later fixed recorded event test alerted on **18 of 19** source-reviewed plush appearances, but fired **17 false events in 190.409 seconds** without plush (5.357/min versus the <0.5/min requirement). All 43 positive crops showed plush, including 25 repeat firings. Sixteen false events targeted one cap, and one targeted a shoe. The first short appearance was missed; 19 appearances are also short of the specified 20 pushes. All 60 emitted crops were independently audited. AP50 is a box metric, and 18/19 is observed recall on these appearances, not general 90% accuracy. Sources: [V4 new-room ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v4-new-room-evaluation-20260927.zip), [V5 development ZIP](../vision/artifacts/rat-litroom-v5-development-candidate-20260927.zip) (`reports/metrics.json`), [fresh box result](../vision/V5_FRESH_RESULTS.md), and [fixed event test](../vision/V5_FORMAL_EVENT_RESULTS.md).
- **Use current, labeled demo media.** The public story currently shows one gallery image and no video link. The [rig photo](assets/physical-prototype.jpeg) shows the physical prototype, and the [live event screenshot](assets/live-pi-map-20260927.png) shows the local map after the accepted Pi event. The [V5 recorded-development montage](v5-montage.md) contains real saved model-output stills, but its closing “fresh test pending” caption is now stale; pair it with the failed formal event result and fresh box-test caveat if shown. The [V4 room-lit review sheet](media-v4/room-lit-review-contact-sheet.jpg) distinguishes manual labels from model predictions. Label replay media as recorded footage, not live Pi or IR detection.
- **Qualify the equity claim.** The income and limited-English contrast describes **modeled silent cells**, not a causal result about residents or a measured rat census. The public page's 17.8× complaint versus 2.3× proactive active-sign comparison is supported by the [validation export](../model/out/validation.json), conditional on inspected lots. Keep that denominator near the claim. The “97% not swept” figure appears in [model documentation](../model/README.md); link or preserve its underlying table before presenting it as a precise public statistic. Add direct study links to the “rats stay local” line if retained.

## Proposed short detector paragraph

"Our physical prototype uses a Pi 5 camera and PIR sensor. We trained YOLO11n on a dark plush rat in **room-lit** footage and exported it to ONNX. During a live Pi camera observation, one genuine plush event reached our local API and moved a map candidate from rank 2 to 1. The model is not ready for a field pilot: its fixed recorded test alerted on 18 of 19 reviewed plush appearances but fired 17 false events on a cap and shoe in 190 seconds. A separate fresh box test did not establish our 0.90 target; the as-run AP50 was 0.616 with known reference-label defects and no corrected score. IR and wild-rat performance remain unverified. We will improve distractor handling and test on new footage.”
