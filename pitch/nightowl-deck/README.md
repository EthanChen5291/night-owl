# NightOwl HTML pitch deck

Open `index.html` in Chrome. The folder is self-contained (fonts and images are local), so it works offline.

- **Keys:** ← → or space to move, **F** fullscreen, **N** speaker notes with the script and timing for each slide, Home / End.
- **Talk:** slides 1–11 are the three-minute pitch (Problem 0:00–0:57, Tech 0:57–1:57, What's next 1:57–2:25, Demo 2:25–3:00). Slides 12–17 are the crew and Q&A backups.
- **Two slides take one extra →:** slide 3 morphs the income pair into the language pair, and slide 8 fades the Owl's specs into the validation loop.
- **Links:** `#8` opens slide 8, `#3.2` opens the second step of slide 3, and `?pdf` hides the navigation chrome.
- **Static copy:** [`NightOwl_Deck.pdf`](NightOwl_Deck.pdf) has 19 pages: the 17 slides plus the second step of slides 3 and 8.

The numbers and how they were computed are in [`model/README.md`](../../model/README.md#pitch-numbers-who-calls-who-gets-inspected-what-rats-signal). The backtest chart is drawn from [`model/out/backtest.json`](../../model/out/backtest.json).

The Owl images on slides 1 and 8 are renders of the node design. The demo uses the real Raspberry Pi 5 Owl. Cost ($30) and battery (up to 6 weeks) are for the low-power production design, not the Pi 5 demo Owl.

Fonts: Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Geist and Geist Mono, all SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Google Fonts).
