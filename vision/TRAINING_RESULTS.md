# Detector training results

The target in this experiment is a dark plush rat moved through lit room scenes recorded by the Barn Owl camera. Codex agents visually reviewed frames and drew boxes around the visible plush body. The labels have no independent human signoff. Infrared recordings were inspected but excluded because object identity was too uncertain to label reliably. These results do not measure detection of live rats or dark-room performance.

Each dataset is grayscale replicated across three channels to match Pi inference. Frame lists and split tags are in the corresponding `dataset/manifest.json` snapshots. The v2 dataset fingerprint in the training report hashes every image/label path and file hash. A full-directory hash, including `data.yaml` and the manifest, is `eea677dc8e35aca3e3fcfdc4c26c67e9f024de6d2ca5d3ccec09a38ce425092a`; the v2 manifest SHA-256 is `ae62db7c06a99e289fa878ab1b6d9804da320b42dd8c4cd1ea2cb1759cbf764c`. The saved v2 snapshot has the same manifest hash as the source dataset. V2's `*_dense` tags contain additional 0.5 fps samples from the four physical training clips; they are not independent recordings. Both v2 validation recordings are absent from training. All included frames have a review marker and a label file; empty files mean no labeled target or person.

V1 ran on a bounded NVIDIA L4 job from pretrained `yolo11n.pt` at 416 pixels, batch 16. It stopped early after 27 of 60 requested epochs, with only four batches per epoch. Raw PyTorch-to-ONNX parity passed on five validation images, but rat AP50 was far below the 0.9 gate and the person class had no training labels. Its candidate was retained for analysis and was not promoted to the Pi. V2 added varied lit positive and negative frames and 20 reviewed person boxes. It ran all 150 requested epochs on a bounded L4 job with batch 8 and a reduced augmentation recipe. Under matched square-416 validation (`rect=False`, batch 1, confidence 0.001, IoU 0.7), PyTorch and ONNX had identical AP50: rat 0.79031 and person 0.84326. Raw tensor parity on five inputs also passed. The initial report compared default rectangular PyTorch validation (rat 0.84353) with square ONNX validation (rat 0.79000); its apparent 0.05353 gap was a validation geometry mismatch, not evidence of a conversion error. Deployment-shape rat AP50 still missed the 0.9 gate, so no v2 candidate was promoted to the Pi.

The per-clip scores use the same square-416 settings for PyTorch and ONNX. The strong close-zoom score conceals weaker generalization on `table_c`. Event-level validation has not run because reviewed push timestamps and a negative reel are still needed. These results do not support the intended unattended alerting behavior.

V3 adds reviewed edge and partial-body poses from the original training clips and 15 more recordings of the lit metal table. Codex agents visually checked every included frame and labeled visible people as well as the plush. The added short recordings come from the same camera, table, and session, so their frames are correlated. They do not supply an independent scene test. The 49 validation image and label files are byte-identical to v2; neither held-out clip entered training. V3's reviewed grayscale dataset has 414 training frames, 259 plush boxes, 227 person boxes, and 94 frames with neither class. The image/label fingerprint is `e72f90416521f5b7ce079735e4d7eb3b79400201fb270c155c20dc0afd71657a`; the manifest SHA-256 is `32149f397d8c0dc9b83bca1ea7f844786dca4fee4864aa6dcd5d2a2f046403bc`. Its full-directory hash is `cd0872dbf3df671f59e00be1ec232848f8f86be2baf295eb573d835ea518a993`. A later pre-test audit found that class-1 seated-person boxes in train clips `short_23`–`short_26` extend into chair fabric. The frozen v3 run retains those broad boxes, which limits interpretation of person AP and person-based suppression.

V3 trained for 150 epochs on the bounded L4 job. The independent CPU verifier measured matching PyTorch and ONNX square-416 AP50: rat 0.943296, person 0.903235, combined 0.923266. Rat AP50 was 0.995 on the reflective metal-table `zoom15_b` clip and 0.817 on the floor `table_c` clip. At Pi runtime confidence 0.5 on the sampled validation frames, it matched 17 of 17 metal-table plush boxes with no unmatched detections, and 9 of 12 floor boxes with one unmatched detection. The ONNX candidate SHA-256 is `3214f2a0dbe1699016b8f4e8aaa8d4c7c5eb3d5e365a07f26a2efbe13114672b`. The run report is under `runs/modal-rat-v3-expanded-20260926/`. The fixed validation clips were used to compare three training recipes and audit 32 checkpoints, so this score is subject to model-selection bias. We reserved later recordings for one evaluation after locking the model and settings. Full original-video replay is still pending.

A separate final-test reserve contains 186 reviewed frames from four recordings kept out of training and recipe selection: a plush-positive metal-table clip, a person-only clip, a later empty-table clip, and a later 111-second clip of the plush moving on a string with a person nearby. The latter two recordings were captured after v3 froze. The reserve has 79 plush boxes, 91 person boxes, and 71 fully empty frames. Two sampled frames were excluded from AP scoring before predictions because the plush boundary was unclear; original-video replay still includes every frame. A pre-test audit added boxes for the visible seated person in the older plush-positive clip and reconciled partial-person labels in the 111-second clip. The empty-table and moving-plush recordings use a changed camera angle, but the table and camera system remain related. Phone-exported MOVs of those recordings are duplicates, not additional test clips. The test-only dataset uses the same grayscale conversion as training. Its frozen manifest SHA-256 is `3d294d414be75741924ae33ae45a22e2363413ad7234ea6288388c89154ba060`; image/label fingerprint is `f94f4a6b24920df6dacf96a05e4a09961a7ebd3cc37d9f1f1d6496beac621ef2`.

The one-time locked-candidate CPU evaluation on that reserve measured identical PyTorch and ONNX rat AP50 of 0.870308 overall and person AP50 of 0.304945. On the new moving-plush recording, rat AP50 was 0.844166 and person AP50 was 0.130979. The older plush-positive recording scored 0.995 rat and 0.910848 person. The person-only and empty-table clips have no rat ground-truth boxes, so rat AP is undefined for them; false-detection and event-replay counts are separate checks. These numbers show a gap between the repeatedly used 0.943296 validation score and the unseen changed-angle footage. The independent verifier report is under `runs/modal-rat-v3-expanded-20260926/final_test_once/`. The four clips are now consumed test evidence and cannot serve as an independent test for a later model trained with them. Full original-video replay remains pending.
