V2's train box loss fell from 1.40 to .36, while validation box loss stayed near .86. Its best combined validation mAP50-95 occurred at epoch 16. The new trial tests faster optimization and early stopping; simply extending the v2 training curve would likely increase overfit.

Trial 1 selected from 21 saved checkpoints. Modal's GPU square-416 rat AP50 was .810984; the independent verifier ran both PT and ONNX on CPU and measured .808733. Use the independent CPU numbers in the comparison table. Metal-table AP fell by .0312 from v2 while floor AP rose by .1191. The verifier also recorded 15/17 matched metal rats and 4/12 matched floor rats at the Pi runtime confidence .5; those are frame detections, not event recall.

Trial 2's rat-only checkpoint improves the metal AP but loses person AP. Its preserved trainer-best checkpoint improves both classes over v2, but still misses the combined rat AP50 target. The trainer-best ONNX was exported locally without simplification because the local environment lacks `onnxslim`; the independent CPU verifier measured identical PT and ONNX AP50 on the fixed protocol. Trial 2 stopped at epoch 43 because Ultralytics early stopping uses combined fitness, while rat AP was still rising. The next run will use full-epoch patience and keep both rat-best and person-qualified checkpoints.

Full original-video replay of the trial-2 trainer-best model produced 59 candidate events over 207 seconds of metal-table video. The detector reviewer confirmed event 2 at 27.528 seconds is a blue book, not the plush. The sampled-frame AP and runtime counts missed this false event. Trial 2 is therefore not a replacement for the delivered v2 demo candidate.

Short clips 32 and 35, plus nearby clips 30, 31, 33, and 34, are excluded from training; the two reviewed reserve clips will be evaluated once after candidate selection is locked.

The independent local CPU verifier measured trial 3 rat AP50 .943296, person AP50 .903235, metal rat AP50 .995, and floor rat AP50 .817. It measured identical PT and ONNX AP50. Modal's CPU image measured rat .945749 and person .904091; the local verifier is the comparison reference used for the earlier trials. At the Pi runtime confidence .5 on sampled validation frames, metal had 17/17 matched rats and no unmatched rats; floor had 9/12 matched and one unmatched. These frame counts do not establish event precision. A later annotation review found some v3 TRAIN person boxes in short clips 23–26 include chair fabric. The frozen run cannot correct those labels, so inspect person suppression during replay.

The detector replayed both full original validation videos through the Pi inference and event rules at confidence .5, floor_y 0, three hits in one second, and two-second cooldown, with no POST. It emitted 63 metal events over 207.36 seconds and 47 floor events over 263.01 seconds. All 110 saved crops were visually reviewed and appear to contain the plush; no blue-book or other-object event was found. The reports and contact sheets are in `runs/modal-rat-v3-expanded-20260926/`. These replays are still on the same clips used for recipe and checkpoint selection, and the videos lack verified push times and a three-minute negative reel. They cannot establish formal event recall or false-event rate.
