## One-time independent test of the locked v3 candidate

The exact ONNX SHA256 `3214f2a0dbe1699016b8f4e8aaa8d4c7c5eb3d5e365a07f26a2efbe13114672b` and PT SHA256 `6a372800a6f02b91c867721eee48a822522d915c4fe2fb8ce869a5274857d38a` were locked before test inference. It contains 186 reviewed frames from four whole recordings outside training and checkpoint selection, including a post-training fresh scene. The verifier checked source-label hashes and split disjointness.

Independent CPU PT and ONNX AP50 matched: **rat .870308 and person .304945 combined**. The fresh positive clip `fresh_204211` scored rat .844166 and person .130979 (111 frames, 67 rat boxes). Metal clip `short_32` scored rat .995 and person .910848 (16 frames, 12 rat boxes). `short_35` has no rat ground truth, so rat AP is undefined; person AP is .285449. `new_scene_203035` has no ground-truth boxes, so both class AP values are undefined. On sampled frames at the locked Pi confidence .5, the fresh clip had 54 matched and 42 unmatched rat proposals against 67 rat boxes; the empty new scene had one unmatched rat proposal. These proposal counts do not measure event false alarms.

The detector replayed every frame of each reserved source video once at the locked runtime settings, without POST. It emitted 7 events in `short_32` (17.13 seconds), 0 in `short_35` (11.93 seconds), 1 in the empty `new_scene_203035` (46.92 seconds), and 39 in `fresh_204211` (111.51 seconds). Native-resolution frame and crop review found the sole empty-scene event is stationary right-edge chair fabric. Fourteen of the 39 fresh-scene events also fire on that chair fabric; the other 25 appear to contain the plush, including event 26 held at the right edge. All seven `short_32` events appear to contain the plush, including event 7 held near hands. The source clips, JSON reports, and event audit sheets are preserved in `runs/modal-rat-v3-expanded-20260926/final_test_once/`.

The independent combined rat AP50 is below the requested .9 target, person AP50 drops sharply in the fresh setting, and the locked event rules produce false chair events. The v3 model is therefore a fixed-validation success and an independent-test failure. These test recordings are now consumed development evidence; they must not be reported as an untouched test for a later model.
